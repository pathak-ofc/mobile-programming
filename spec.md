Nepal Esports Platform

Problem Statement
Gaming and esports are growing rapidly in Nepal, especially games like Valorant, PUBG Mobile, and Free Fire. Players currently find scrims and tournaments through different Discord servers, Facebook groups, and other platforms. This makes it difficult to find competitive opportunities in one place. Tournament organizers also have to manage registrations, teams, schedules, brackets, and results using different tools, making the process time-consuming and unorganized.

Objective
The objective is to create one centralized platform for Nepal's esports community where players, teams, and tournament organizers can connect and compete easily. Players can find and book scrims, join tournaments, find teams, and build their gaming profiles. Teams can manage their players and competitions, while organizers can create and manage tournaments, registrations, matches, and results in one place.
